Under the hood

How the beat sync works

Polling an AnalyserNode every frame ties beat detection to the render frame rate, which falls apart on a slow device. Dance Fight does it offline instead, on the decoded audio buffer, before the fight starts.

The offline analysis

  1. Low-pass the signal to ~180 Hz with a one-pole filter (kick-drum territory).
  2. Build a per-10 ms RMS envelope, then take its positive flux.
  3. Peak-pick onsets against an adaptive threshold.
  4. Autocorrelate the flux over lags of 0.30–0.75 s to find the tempo, refined to sub-sample accuracy.
  5. Search every phase offset for the grid that lines up with the most onset energy.

Locked to the playhead

The result is a beat grid. During play the dance phase is read straight off the audio playhead — (playhead − phase) / period — so the groove cannot drift, and it is completely independent of frame rate.

Everything else is drawn on a forward-kinematic skeleton. Moves are keyframed joint angles blended over the ongoing dance groove rather than replacing it. Spins are faked as a horizontal squash that mirrors past 90°, with near and far limbs swapping, and every attack leaves additive ghost trails of the last few poses — which is what makes a rotation read as motion.

Good questions

FAQ

QDo I need to install anything?▾

No. Dance Fight is a single HTML file with zero dependencies — no build step, no framework, no CDN. Open it in a modern browser and play, and it works offline and from file:// too.

QCan I use my own music?▾

Yes. Load an audio file from your device and the game analyses it for its real beat grid. If you just want to start, press Start and it plays a default 128 BPM groove.

QWhy do I hear nothing at first?▾

Browsers block audio until you interact with the page, so your first tap or click unlocks sound. If you ever hear nothing, a live level meter under the BPM readout shows whether audio is actually flowing — and a banner appears if it isn’t. That meter reads real signal, not a flag.

QHow does the timing work?▾

A move thrown within about 80 ms of a beat lands as a PERFECT: 1.6× damage and a bigger combo multiplier. The gold · ON BEAT tag on the move name tells you when you nailed it. Shuffle-stepping in time charges your finisher meter.

QDoes it work on a phone?▾

Yes — on-screen controls appear automatically. Hold a direction with one thumb and tap attacks with the other. The arena is 16:9, so rotate to landscape; it’s playable in portrait but cramped.

QAre the spins and trails frame-rate dependent?▾

No. Beat detection is done offline on the decoded audio buffer, and during play the dance phase is read straight off the audio playhead — so the groove can’t drift and is independent of frame rate. One honest caveat: the geometry, timing and beat grid were verified programmatically, but the feel of the spins and trails in motion was only tested in a headless browser rendering a few frames per second — that part is the player’s call.

QCan I fork and remix it?▾

Absolutely. It’s MIT licensed © Dr Sohil Momin. It’s a single HTML file, so fork it, remix it, and drop your own track in.
